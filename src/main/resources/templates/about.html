WELCOME TO SSTS

About us

Welcome to Sandy Springs Tamil School! Our school is run by a dedicated group of volunteers from the greater Atlanta area who are passionate about promoting the Tamil language to the next generation. Inspired by the timeless words of Kaniyan Poongundranar, “யாதும் ஊரே யாவரும் கேளிர்” ("All places are our own; all people are our kin"), we carry our precious language, culture, and traditions wherever we go. Tamil is one of the world's oldest and most enduring classical languages, with a literary heritage spanning over 2,000 years. We are honored to be a small part of this vast tradition and are committed to passing on our language skills to the younger generation.

Our primary goal is to help our children speak Tamil confidently and without hesitation. We introduce phonetic methods in line with grammar and vocabulary, starting with simple words and gradually building up to forming sentences, engaging in conversations, debating, and reciting. We emphasize learning Tamil through games, conversations, songs, and fun activities to spark interest in a lively classroom environment. Whether a child is a complete beginner or understands Tamil but struggles with speaking, our curriculum, combined with ample practice, will ensure they gain the confidence and skills needed to speak Tamil fluently.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission, Vision & Foundation

Our mission

Build Tamil language skills through joyful, age-appropriate learning so every child gains confidence speaking, reading, and writing Tamil.

Our vision

Help young learners stay connected to their Tamil heritage, curiosity, and community well beyond the classroom.

Our foundation

SSTS was founded in the 2024-2025 academic year by a board of members that includes Mr. Jayasarathi M and Mr. Mahendiran Manickam. We began our journey with 30 students and 8 teachers.

Affiliation

This program has been affiliated with American Tamil Academy and their syllabus.

Over the years, that circle has grown into a welcoming community of teachers, coordinators, and families from across the Atlanta metro. We are rooted in the traditions of Tamil Nadu and the diaspora, and we meet each Friday to learn, celebrate, and belong together.

All instruction is in English and Tamil, and families span every background — from first-generation learners to fluent speakers keeping the language alive abroad.
